FAQ

Good to know.

What happens if it snows on my scheduled day? +

We provide year-round service, including through Minnesota winters. If fresh snowfall makes part of your yard inaccessible, some areas may be missed during that visit — in that case, we'll offer a refund or credit for the missed area.

How does billing work? +

You're billed monthly, in advance — we send an invoice for the upcoming month before your next round of visits begins.

What if I'm not happy with a visit? +

Your satisfaction is guaranteed. If a visit doesn't meet expectations, let us know and we'll make it right with a refund or credit.

Do I need to be home, or do anything after my first visit? +

No — once your schedule is set up, service runs automatically. It's a reliable, contact-free routine: we show up on your chosen schedule, and you'll get a text with photo confirmation after every visit.
