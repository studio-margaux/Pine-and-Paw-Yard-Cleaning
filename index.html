FAQ

Good to know.

Can I skip a visit or pause service? +

Yes — skip a week, pause for a vacation, or cancel anytime. No fees, no penalties. Just let us know.

What happens if it snows on my scheduled day? +

We provide year-round service, including through Minnesota winters. If fresh snowfall makes part of your yard inaccessible, some areas may be missed during that visit — in that case, we'll offer a refund or credit for the missed area.

Do you skip visits when it rains? +

Light rain isn't a problem — we keep our route running as scheduled. In severe weather, we pause the route and resume once it clears, which may shift your visit slightly that day.

What if my dog is aggressive or doesn't do well with strangers in the yard? +

For our insurance coverage, all dogs need to be kept inside or away from the area being serviced during your visit. Just let us know ahead of time and we'll work around your dog's routine.

How does billing work? +

You're billed monthly, in advance — we send an invoice for the upcoming month before your next round of visits begins.

What payment methods do you accept? +

We accept credit and debit cards, as well as Venmo.

What if there's a lot of buildup since I haven't had this done in a while? +

No problem — we'll treat your first visit as a One-Time Cleaning to get your yard fully caught up, then your regular plan pricing kicks in starting with your next scheduled visit.

What if I'm not happy with a visit? +

Your satisfaction is guaranteed. If a visit doesn't meet expectations, let us know and we'll make it right with a refund or credit.

Do I need to be home, or do anything after my first visit? +

No — once your schedule is set up, service runs automatically. It's a reliable, contact-free routine: we show up on your chosen schedule, and you'll get a text with photo confirmation after every visit.

Do you offer a referral discount? +

Yes — refer a friend and you'll both get $10 off a recurring monthly plan once they sign up. It's a one-time thank-you per referral.
