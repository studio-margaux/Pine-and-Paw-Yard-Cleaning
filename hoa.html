Two ways to work together

Pick the model that fits your community.

Many associations run both at once — common areas covered by the board, individual yards discounted for residents.

Model A

Community-paid service

We service shared green space, walking trails, dog parks, and waste stations — billed directly to the association, like landscaping. No resident involvement needed.

  • Billed monthly, fits your budget cycle
  • Waste station bin emptying & bag restocking
  • 60–90 day minimum, no long-term lock-in
Model B

Resident discount program

We become your preferred vendor and offer residents 10–20% off standard pricing for their own yards — promoted through your newsletter or board communications.

  • Zero cost to the association
  • Reduces resident complaints about neighbors' yards
  • Especially popular in 55+ communities
